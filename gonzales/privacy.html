Gonzales Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-05

Gonzales is a macOS app that helps users move the cursor between displays. Gonzales does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

Data Collection

Gonzales does not collect personal information, usage analytics, crash analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data.

Data Sharing

Since no personal data is collected, no personal data is sold or shared with third parties.

Permissions

Gonzales may request system permissions required for its cursor-related functionality. These permissions are processed by macOS and are not used by us to collect personal data.

Children's Privacy

Gonzales is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
